Kemi:Posts, Comments & Reactions
Research Summary: How Users Share and Interact with Content
Based on current social media practices, here's a comprehensive overview:

Creating a Post
Access the platform and click "What's on your mind?" (Facebook), "Tweet" (X), or similar buttons
Add content: text, images, videos, links, hashtags, tags, or location info
Set audience/privacy level before publishing
Click "Post," "Share," or "Tweet" to publish
Editing a Post
Locate the post on your profile or timeline
Click menu (three dots or dropdown) and select "Edit post"
Revise text, adjust media, or update tags
Save changes to update the post
Note: Not all platforms allow editing (e.g., X/Twitter only with Premium), and edits may not be available for all content types
Deleting a Post
Use the built-in "delete" or "remove" function from the post menu
Content is removed from public view, though platforms may retain backups
Important: Deletion doesn't always erase data from search caches or third-party backups
For permanent account deletion, be aware this differs from deactivation
Comments
Users can respond to posts with text messages, questions, or opinions
Comments create discussions and allow engagement beyond simple reactions
Can typically be edited, deleted, or filtered based on privacy settings
Allow threaded replies on most modern platforms
Likes/Reactions
Likes: Basic positive approval (thumbs-up, heart icon)
Reactions: Nuanced emotional responses (Facebook offers: Like, Love, Haha, Wow, Sad, Angry)
Interactions (Broad Term): Encompasses all engagement—likes, comments, shares, saves, watching videos, clicking links
Viewing Posts
Posts appear in user feeds/timelines based on algorithm or chronological order
Users can search for specific posts or accounts
Tagged posts may require approval before appearing on your profile
Activity status shows who's currently online (can be hidden)
Basic Post Privacy Controls
Audience Options:

Public (anyone can see)
Friends/Followers only
Custom/Selected groups
Only me (private)
Profile Privacy: Public or private account settings

Sharing Controls: Allow/block others from sharing your posts

Comment Controls: Filter who can comment (Everyone, Friends, No One)

Tag Management: Approve tags before they appear on your profile

Story/Status Privacy: Control visibility separately from regular posts

Key Best Practices
✓ Regularly review privacy settings
✓ Think before posting (deletion isn't always complete)
✓ Use strong passwords and 2FA
✓ Block or report unwanted contacts
✓ Periodically audit posts and connections
✓ Understand that "deleted" content may remain in backups or search caches

This framework is consistent across platforms like Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and TikTok, though specific features and terminology vary by platform.
